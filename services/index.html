Our Dental Services

From routine cleanings to implants, Cosmo Dental Clinic offers complete dental care for the whole family in Northlake, IL.

  • General Dentistry

    Preventive and routine dental care for children and adults, focused on keeping your teeth and gums healthy for life.

    Treatments include

    • Check-ups, exams and professional cleanings
    • Digital X-rays
    • Cavity detection and tooth-colored fillings
    • Gum disease treatment

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Modern cosmetic treatments tailored to each patient to brighten, reshape and refresh your smile.

    Treatments include

    • Teeth whitening
    • Porcelain veneers
    • Smile makeover planning

  • Dental Implants

    Permanent tooth replacement that restores the look, comfort and function of natural teeth.

    Treatments include

    • Single tooth implants
    • Implant-supported bridges
    • Full-mouth restoration

  • Endodontics (Root Canal Treatment)

    Root canal treatment to relieve tooth pain, treat infection and save your natural tooth.

    Treatments include

    • Root canal therapy
    • Root canal retreatment
    • Urgent tooth pain relief

  • Pediatric Dentistry

    Gentle, friendly dental care designed to help children feel comfortable and keep their smiles healthy.

    Treatments include

    • Kid-friendly check-ups and cleanings
    • Fluoride treatments and dental sealants
    • Tooth-colored fillings for baby and adult teeth
    • Tips for brushing, flossing and healthy snacks

Not sure which treatment you need?

Book a check-up and your dentist will explain your options.
